> still outer
>
> > inner quote still inner
>
> - quoted list
> - quoted next
>   - nested in the quote

A quoted fence, and the bare `>` that
ends it. The grammar hands that marker
to the fence as its last child, so the
line break belongs to the fence rule and
not to whatever follows:

> before the fence
>
> ```json
> { "quoted": true }
> ```
>
> after the fence

> ```sh
> echo 'a fence that opens the quote'
> ```
>
> and still in it

A quoted list followed by a paragraph;
the paragraph's marker belongs to the
last list item in the tree:

> - one
> - two
>
> after

The same boundary inside another quote:

> > - inner one
> > - inner two
> >
> > after the inner list

A list followed by a heading in its
quote:

> - before the heading
>
> ## Still quoted
>
> after the heading
